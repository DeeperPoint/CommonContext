# Haas VF-2 Vertical Machining Center — Specifications

The Haas VF-2 is a 40-taper vertical machining center and, per Haas's own product materials, the most popular model in the VF series — often described as "the workhorse of any shop." It is a commonly cited reference machine for mid-size CNC shop capacity in North America.

## Travel & Table

- **Travels (X/Y/Z):** 762 × 406 × 508 mm (30.00 × 16.00 × 20.00 in)
- **Spindle nose to table:** 102–610 mm (4.0–24.0 in)
- **Table size:** 914 × 356 mm (36.0 × 14.0 in)
- **T-slots:** 3, on 125 mm (4.92 in) centers
- **Table load limit:** 1,361 kg (3,000 lb)

## Spindle

- **Type:** Inline direct-drive
- **Power:** 22.4 kW (30.0 hp)
- **Max speed:** 8,100 rpm
- **Torque:** 122.0 Nm (90.0 ft-lbf) at 2,000 rpm
- **Taper:** 40-taper (CT/BT-40 tooling)

## Tooling & Motion

- **Tool changer:** 20-station carousel
- **Rapids:** 25.4 m/min (1,000 in/min)
- **Rigid tapping:** yes

## Control & Utility

- 15" color LCD monitor, USB, Ethernet
- 1 GB program memory
- Power-failure detection module
- Memory lock keyswitch
- Flood coolant, 208 L (55 gal) tank

## Why this machine matters for a capacity-exchange marketplace

The VF-2 is a good reference point for "standard" 3-axis mid-size CNC capacity — a shop advertising VF-2-class capability signals: 40-taper tooling compatibility, work envelope under ~760×410×510 mm, and mid-range spindle speed/torque suited to steel and aluminum work (not the high-temp superalloy work that calls for 5-axis, higher-rigidity machines — see `mazak_variaxis_5axis_spec.md`).
