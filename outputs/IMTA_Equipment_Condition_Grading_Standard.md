## **IMTA Equipment Condition Grading Standard No. 12** 

## **(2025)** 

_International Machinery Traders Association — Reference standard for classifying the condition of used and refurbished industrial machinery offered on trading platforms._ 

## **1. Purpose** 

This Standard defines the controlled condition vocabulary used by Sellers when listing machinery and by inspectors when certifying it. Consistent grading reduces information opacity between Buyer and Seller in markets where units are geographically dispersed and cannot be co-inspected.

## **3. Inspection Dimensions** 

An inspection certificate shall record the following dimensions: operating 

hours, structural integrity, hydraulic condition, engine emissions tier, tyre/track wear percentage, and presence of original documentation. Each dimension is scored Pass, Conditional, or Fail. 

## **4. Machinery Categories Covered** 

Construction; Agriculture; Manufacturing / CNC; Material Handling; Mining; Power Generation; Forestry; Oil & Gas. 

## **5. Emissions Classification** 

Diesel-powered units shall declare an emissions standard: EPA Tier 2, EPA Tier 3, EPA Tier 4, or EU Stage V. Units failing to meet the destination market's minimum tier may be restricted from import. 

## **6. Certifications Referenced** 

ISO 9001 (Quality Management); CE Marking (EU conformity); OSHA Compliant (US workplace safety); EPA Tier 4 (emissions). A unit's listing should declare which certifications apply.
