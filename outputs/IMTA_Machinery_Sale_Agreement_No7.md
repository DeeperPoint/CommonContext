## **IMTA Contract No. 7** 

_International Machinery Traders Association — Standard Agreement for the Sale of Used & Refurbished Industrial Machinery — CIF / FOB / EXW Terms — 2025 Edition_ 

_Governing Law: Laws of England  •  Arbitration: IMTA Arbitration Rules No. 41_ 

## **Clause 1 — Parties** 

This Agreement is made between the **Seller** (the party offering machinery for sale — being a dealer, original equipment manufacturer, auction house, or end-user disposing of surplus assets) and the **Buyer** (the party purchasing the machinery — being a contractor, manufacturer, mining operator, rental fleet, government body, or reseller). The Seller and Buyer may appoint a **Service Provider** (transport carrier, rigging specialist, inspector, or customs broker) to facilitate inspection, dismantling, and delivery of the Goods.

## **Clause 4 — Price and Payment** 

The Price is expressed per unit in United States Dollars (USD). Payment shall 

be by irrevocable letter of credit or escrow release against inspection 

certificate. Typical per-unit value bands are: under 25k, 25k–100k, 100k–500k, 500k–2M, and 2M and above. 

## **Clause 5 — Delivery Terms** 

Delivery shall be on EXW, FOB, or CIF terms per INCOTERMS 2020. The Seller shall make the unit available for pre-shipment inspection. Cross-border movements require a commercial invoice, packing list, and where applicable a certificate of origin and customs declaration. 

## **Clause 6 — Inspection and Warranties** 

The Buyer is entitled to appoint an independent inspector to verify operating hours, structural integrity, and compliance with the stated condition designation. For Refurbished units the Seller shall provide a certificate of refurbishment. 

## **Clause 7 — Compliance** 

Machinery shall, where applicable, conform to ISO 9001 quality management, CE Marking (EU), OSHA workplace-safety requirements (US), and EPA Tier 4 emissions standards for diesel-powered equipment. 

## **Clause 8 — Origin Countries** 

The Goods may originate from the United States, Canada, Mexico, Germany, Italy, Japan, China, South Korea, India, or Brazil.
